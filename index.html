Product Manager

Hi, I'm Rohan Pandit

Product strategist and engineer at heart, building the bridge between complex systems and the people who rely on them.

I love to build. I started as an application developer before moving into product, an engineering foundation that still shapes how I think about systems, not just a roadmap. In 8+ years at Optum Financial, Starship, and Engine Room, I've led product builds across fintech, healthcare, and ecommerce for millions of consumers.

Most recently, I've applied that same drive to AI: shipping agentic systems with tool-calling, multi-agent orchestration, RAG pipelines, and guardrails/evals, because I wanted to prove I could build it, not just talk about it.

The problems I find most exciting are the ones without a playbook yet — new technology, real consequences, and not a clear consensus on the right move.
